# Microsoft

Microsoft is one of the three dominant quantum hardware companies (alongside [[google-quantum-ai]] and [[ibm]]), pursuing a distinctive hardware strategy based on topological qubits rather than [[superconducting-qubits]].

## Quantum Computing Approach

Microsoft's Azure Quantum platform provides cloud access to quantum hardware and simulators. Unlike IBM and Google, Microsoft's long-term hardware bet is on **topological qubits** — a hardware modality that promises inherently lower error rates by encoding quantum information in non-local physical states, potentially reducing the overhead of [[quantum-error-correction]].

## Role in Quantum AI

Cited in [[qubits-to-insights-quantum-ai-2026]] as one of the hardware providers enabling enterprise Quantum AI exploration in 2026. Azure Quantum supports [[hybrid-classical-quantum-computing]] workflows alongside classical Azure cloud services.
