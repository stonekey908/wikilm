# Google Quantum AI

Google's quantum computing research division, headquartered in Santa Barbara, California. Responsible for Google's 2019 quantum supremacy claim (Sycamore processor, random circuit sampling) and a sustained program of superconducting qubit hardware development and quantum algorithm research.

## Key Research Areas

- Superconducting qubit hardware (Sycamore, Willow processors).
- Quantum error correction and fault tolerance.
- Quantum simulation and quantum chemistry.
- Quantum machine learning — including the 2026 ML memory advantage study ([[exponential-quantum-advantage-ml-tasks]]).
- Physics simulation at scale — in October 2025 Google demonstrated a **13,000× speedup** over the Frontier supercomputer using 65 qubits for physics simulations (cited in [[quantum-computing-ai-2026-guide-bqpsim]]).

## Role in 2026 ML Advantage Study

Multiple Google Quantum AI researchers (Haimeng Zhao, Hartmut Neven, Ryan Babbush, Jarrod R. McClean) co-authored arXiv:2604.07639, demonstrating exponential quantum memory advantage for classification, dimensionality reduction, and linear equation solving using [[quantum-oracle-sketching]] and [[classical-shadow-tomography]].
