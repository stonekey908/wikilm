# IBM

IBM is one of the three dominant quantum hardware companies (alongside [[google-quantum-ai]] and [[microsoft]]) building superconducting qubit processors and making them accessible via cloud services.

## Quantum Computing Efforts

IBM's quantum platform (IBM Quantum / IBM Quantum Network) provides cloud access to [[superconducting-qubits]]-based processors. IBM has been a consistent leader in qubit count milestones and is a major driver of the Qiskit open-source quantum SDK.

IBM pursues a roadmap toward fault-tolerant quantum computing, with [[quantum-error-correction]] as a central long-term investment.

## Role in Quantum AI

Cited in [[qubits-to-insights-quantum-ai-2026]] as one of the core hardware providers enabling enterprise Quantum AI exploration in 2026. IBM's cloud infrastructure supports [[hybrid-classical-quantum-computing]] deployments where classical and quantum workloads run in concert.
