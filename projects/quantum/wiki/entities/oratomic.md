# Oratomic

A quantum computing startup co-founded by [[john-preskill]] and [[hsin-yuan-huang]], both of Caltech. The company's research focus aligns with Preskill and Huang's academic programs — quantum algorithms, quantum ML, and techniques like [[classical-shadow-tomography]].

## What Is Known

- Co-founders: [[john-preskill]] and [[hsin-yuan-huang]].
- Appears as an institutional affiliation for Preskill and Huang on the 2026 ML advantage paper ([[exponential-quantum-advantage-ml-tasks]]).
- No public product or hardware offering described in available sources as of April 2026.

## Role in 2026 ML Advantage Study

Oratomic is listed as an affiliation for [[hsin-yuan-huang]] and [[john-preskill]] on arXiv:2604.07639, indicating the company has research interests in quantum memory efficiency and quantum ML.
