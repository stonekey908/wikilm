# John Preskill

Richard P. Feynman Professor of Theoretical Physics at Caltech and co-founder of [[oratomic]]. One of the most influential figures in theoretical quantum computing. Known for coining the term **quantum supremacy** (now largely replaced by "[[quantum-advantage]]") and for the long-running Caltech quantum information course and lecture notes widely used in the field.

## Key Contributions

- Coined "quantum supremacy" (2012) to describe the milestone where a quantum computer performs a task beyond classical reach.
- Developed foundational work on quantum error correction, quantum fault tolerance, and topological quantum computation.
- Co-developed **classical shadow tomography** with [[hsin-yuan-huang]] and collaborators — a technique for efficiently extracting classical information from quantum states ([[classical-shadow-tomography]]).
- Co-founded [[oratomic]], a quantum computing startup, with Huang.

## Role in 2026 ML Advantage Study

Co-author on arXiv:2604.07639 ([[exponential-quantum-advantage-ml-tasks]]), which demonstrates exponential quantum memory advantage for ML tasks including classification and dimensionality reduction. Affiliated via Caltech and [[oratomic]].
