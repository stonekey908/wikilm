# Hsin-Yuan Huang

Quantum computing researcher at Caltech and co-founder of [[oratomic]]. Best known for developing **classical shadow tomography** ([[classical-shadow-tomography]]) — a technique that extracts useful information from quantum states using exponentially fewer measurements than full state tomography. A central figure in quantum machine learning theory.

## Key Contributions

- Developed [[classical-shadow-tomography]] (with [[john-preskill]] and collaborators, ~2020), enabling efficient property estimation of quantum states.
- Co-authored the 2026 study demonstrating exponential quantum memory advantage for ML tasks ([[exponential-quantum-advantage-ml-tasks]]).
- Co-founded [[oratomic]] with [[john-preskill]].

## Role in 2026 ML Advantage Study

Co-author (affiliated via Caltech and Oratomic) on arXiv:2604.07639. His classical shadow tomography work is directly applied in the pipeline demonstrated by the paper.
