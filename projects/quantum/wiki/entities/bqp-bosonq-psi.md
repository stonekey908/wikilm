# BQP (BosonQ Psi)

BosonQ Psi (BQP) is a quantum computing company offering quantum-enhanced AI and simulation tools without requiring direct access to quantum hardware. Their product suite centers on **BQPhy®**, a platform combining quantum-inspired optimization, hybrid quantum-classical workflows, and physics-informed neural networks for engineering and scientific computing.

## Products & Capabilities

| Product | Function |
|---------|----------|
| BQPhy® Optimization Solver | Quantum-Inspired Optimization (QIO) — up to 20× faster than classical methods |
| BQPhy® Physics Solver | [[physics-informed-neural-networks]] (PINNs) + QA-PINN variant for CFD and simulation |
| BQPhy® Data Driven Solver | Data-driven ML workflows with quantum-assisted preprocessing |
| BQPhy® Cloud Platform | Cloud access to hybrid quantum-classical experimentation |

## Key Performance Claims (2026)

- **25× speedup** in CFD training using QA-PINN (Quantum-Assisted Physics-Informed Neural Network) vs. classical PINN baseline.
- **100× circuit compression** achieved in a collaboration with [[classiq]] and [[nvidia]] on quantum computational fluid dynamics (QCFD).
- QIO algorithms reach up to 20× speed advantage over classical optimization for engineering design problems.

## Approach

BQP positions itself as a bridge technology: customers get quantum advantage benefits (faster optimization, compressed circuit representations) via quantum-inspired and hybrid methods while waiting for fault-tolerant hardware to mature. The company integrates with existing HPC/GPU workflows and tools such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, MATLAB, and Ansys.

## Industry Context

BQP is an example of the "quantum-assisted classical AI" layer of the [[hybrid-classical-quantum-computing]] stack — the practical commercialization tier below full [[quantum-machine-learning]]. Their market timing argument: early adopters gain a 3–5 year head start in quantum talent, infrastructure, and algorithm development relative to wait-and-see competitors.
