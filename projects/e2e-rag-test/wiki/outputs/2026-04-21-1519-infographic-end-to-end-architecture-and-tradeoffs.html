Key Insights from the Literature

Naive → Advanced → Modular: Three Generations

Gupta et al. trace RAG as a historical progression. Naive RAG (simple retrieve-then-generate) exposed retrieval quality limits, motivating Advanced RAG's pre/post-retrieval optimisations, then Modular RAG's fully reconfigurable pipelines — now the dominant paradigm in production.

Blinkered Chunk Effect — The Named Failure Mode

Klesel & Wittmann coin this term for the context collapse that happens when a chunk is stripped from its surrounding document. A correctly retrieved chunk can still cause hallucination if the LLM cannot reconstruct the conditions, jargon, or references that gave it meaning.

Hybrid Systems Dominate Complex Tasks

On multi-hop QA (HotpotQA), RQ-RAG achieves 800%+ improvement over raw LLM. IM-RAG adds +5.3 F1 / +7.2 EM. Tightly coupled hybrid systems outperform modular pipelines on complex tasks, though at a cost to interpretability and deployability.

GraphRAG Supplies Relational Context

By extracting entity-relationship knowledge graphs from source corpora, GraphRAG enables multi-hop traversal that pure vector similarity cannot achieve. Dual-Pathway KG-RAG reduces hallucinations 18%; KRAGEN reduces them 20–30% versus text-only RAG baselines.

From sources/rag-comprehensive-survey-2506-00054 · concepts/graphrag

RAG Is Not a Hallucination Silver Bullet

Gupta et al. warn that RAG can amplify corpus bias — if the underlying knowledge store is skewed or noisy, retrieval surfaces that skew reliably. Adversarial corpus poisoning (BadRAG, TrojanRAG) represents a further attack surface with sparse defences as of 2025.

From sources/rag-comprehensive-survey-2410-12837 · concepts/adversarial-rag-attacks

Data Governance Is First-Class Infrastructure

Klesel & Wittmann are the only surveyed authors to frame data quality and governance as a first-class AI infrastructure concern — not a precondition assumed away. Their research agenda asks whether RAG measurably improves organisational performance; no empirical answer exists yet.
