# Shailja Gupta

Researcher and co-author of a comprehensive survey on [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] (arXiv 2410.12837, October 2024). The survey traces RAG's evolution through the Naive → Advanced → Modular paradigm progression and reviews applications, challenges, and future directions as of Q4 2024.
