# Chaitanya Sharma

Independent researcher based in the United States. Author of a comprehensive survey on [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] (arXiv 2506.00054v1, preprint under review at ACM TOIS).

## Work

Sharma's survey synthesizes the RAG landscape as of mid-2025, organizing it into a four-way architectural taxonomy (retriever-centric, generator-centric, hybrid, robustness-oriented) and conducting systematic comparative analysis across short-form QA, [[multi-hop-reasoning]], and robustness benchmarks.

## Key Contributions

- Taxonomy of RAG architectures by locus of innovation
- Comparative performance analysis across 20+ RAG systems on standard benchmarks
- Synthesis of evaluation frameworks (ARES, RAGAS, RAGTruth, RGB, etc.)
- Identification of three core trade-offs in RAG design: precision/flexibility, efficiency/faithfulness, modularity/coordination
