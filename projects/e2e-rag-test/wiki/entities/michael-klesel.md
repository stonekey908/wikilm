# Michael Klesel

Researcher at Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences. Co-authored the BISE Catchword article on [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] (with [[h-felix-wittmann]]), which introduced RAG concepts and research agenda to the Business & Information Systems Engineering community.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)", BISE Vol. 67(4), 2025 (→ [[retrieval-augmented-generation-klesel-wittmann-2025]])
- Introduced the concept of the [[blinkered-chunk-effect]] as a named limitation of plain vanilla RAG architectures

## Affiliation

Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences, Germany.
