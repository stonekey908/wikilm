# Best LLMs 2026 (Zapier)

## Overview

Zapier's roundup evaluates 21 major large language models across proprietary and open/open-source categories. The article prioritises significance, interestingness, and popularity over raw benchmark scores — favouring practical accessibility and real-world capability.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Practical accessibility (API or chatbot availability)
- [[multimodal-llms]] capabilities
- [[reasoning-models]] abilities
- Context window size
- Parameter efficiency
- Open vs. proprietary licensing
- Enterprise suitability

## Key Takeaways

1. **Open models now match proprietary** — DeepSeek, Qwen 3.5, and others achieve parity with closed models while being cheaper to develop.
2. **[[mixture-of-experts]] dominates flagship models** — MoE architecture is near-universal in 2026's largest models, making raw parameter counts misleading.
3. **[[reasoning-models]] are table stakes** — most major releases now support extended reasoning, trading latency for accuracy on complex tasks.
4. **Geographic divide** — Western companies dominate proprietary models; Chinese tech companies (DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot, MiniMax, Xiaomi, Z.ai) dominate open model development.
5. **Claude 4.7 notable for enterprise adoption** — Slack, Notion, and Zoom integrations cited as evidence of production-grade reliability.
