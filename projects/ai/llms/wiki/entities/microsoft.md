# Microsoft

Technology company; develops the Phi small language model (SLM) family alongside its broader [[openai]] investment.

## Key Models (2026)

- **Phi** — family of 3B–15B parameter models; some variants include [[reasoning-models]] capabilities; notable for strong performance at small scale

## What Sources Say

- [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]]: Phi listed as an open/open-source model notable for small parameter count with reasoning support — relevant for on-device and edge deployment scenarios

## Connections

- Microsoft is a major investor in [[openai]]; Phi is its independent open-weight research line
- Phi competes with [[google-deepmind]] Gemma 3 at small parameter scales
- Small model size positions Phi for contexts where [[openai]] GPT-5.4 would be impractical
