# OpenAI

AI research lab and product company. Creator of the GPT model family and the o-series reasoning models.

## Key Models (2026)

- **GPT-5.4** — flagship proprietary model; combines GPT-4o, o3, and Codex into a single multimodal, reasoning-capable general-purpose model
- **gpt-oss** — open-weight release; 21B–117B parameters; supports reasoning

## What Sources Say

- [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]] ranks GPT-5.4 among the top proprietary models; notable for unifying prior model lines
- Enterprise integrations widely cited; ChatGPT remains the most recognised AI product
