# DeepSeek

Chinese AI lab known for achieving state-of-the-art LLM performance with unusually efficient training — less compute and less capital than Western competitors.

## Key Models (2026)

- **DeepSeek R1** — reasoning-focused model; 671B parameters via [[mixture-of-experts]]
- **DeepSeek V3.2** — general-purpose successor; 671B (MoE)

## What Sources Say

- [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]]: "achieved state-of-the-art performance using more limited computer hardware and less financial investment than typical LLMs" — a headline finding that challenged assumptions about the compute required for frontier models
- Qwen 3.5 Max cited as matching or exceeding DeepSeek V3.2 — signals the Chinese open-model tier is extremely competitive internally

## Connections

- Part of the broader Chinese open-model ecosystem alongside [[alibaba-qwen]], Moonshot AI (Kimi K2.5), MiniMax, Xiaomi (MiMo), Z.ai (GLM-5)
- Uses [[mixture-of-experts]] and [[reasoning-models]] architectures
- Directly competes on benchmarks with [[openai]] gpt-oss and [[meta-ai]] Llama 4
- Cited as proof that compute efficiency can substitute for raw capital
