# Meta AI

Meta's AI research division; develops the Llama open-weight model family.

## Key Models (2026)

- **Llama 4** — flagship open-weight model; up to 2 trillion parameters via [[mixture-of-experts]] (MoE) architecture; [[multimodal-llms]]

## What Sources Say

- [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]]: Llama 4 cited as one of the largest open-weight models by parameter count; MoE makes direct parameter comparisons with dense models misleading
- Meta's open-release strategy has driven widespread adoption and fine-tuning across the research community

## Connections

- Competes in the open-weight tier with [[deepseek]], [[alibaba-qwen]], [[mistral-ai]], and [[google-deepmind]] Gemma
- Uses [[mixture-of-experts]] — same architectural choice as DeepSeek, Qwen 3.5 Max, and others
- Llama 4 is [[multimodal-llms]]
