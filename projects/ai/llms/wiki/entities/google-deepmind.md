# Google DeepMind

Google's consolidated AI research organisation; develops both proprietary (Gemini) and open-weight (Gemma) model families.

## Key Models (2026)

- **Gemini 3.1** — flagship proprietary model; multimodal; 1M token context window
- **Gemma 3** — open-weight model family; 270M–27B parameters

## What Sources Say

- [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]]: Gemini 3.1 listed among top proprietary models; 1M context window is a key differentiator
- Gemma 3 is the open-weight counterpart, competing with [[meta-ai]] Llama 4 and [[alibaba-qwen]] Qwen 3.5 at smaller parameter scales

## Connections

- Proprietary Gemini competes with [[openai]] GPT-5.4 and [[anthropic]] Claude 4.7
- Gemma 3 competes in the open-weight tier alongside [[meta-ai]], [[deepseek]], [[mistral-ai]]
- Gemini 3.1 is [[multimodal-llms]] with extended context windows
