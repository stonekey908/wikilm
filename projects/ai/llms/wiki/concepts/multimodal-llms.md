# Multimodal LLMs

Language models that process and generate content across multiple modalities — most commonly text + images, but increasingly text + images + audio + video.

## 2026 Landscape

Multimodal capability is now standard for proprietary flagship models, per [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]]:

**Proprietary multimodal:**
- [[openai]] GPT-5.4
- [[anthropic]] Claude 4.7
- [[google-deepmind]] Gemini 3.1

**Open-weight multimodal:**
- [[meta-ai]] Llama 4

## Why It Matters

Multimodal models unlock use cases impossible with text-only LLMs:
- Document understanding (PDFs, screenshots, diagrams)
- Image Q&A and visual reasoning
- Code generation from mockups
- Medical imaging analysis
- Video understanding (emerging)

## Evaluation Dimensions

Unlike pure text models, multimodal models are evaluated on:
- Visual question answering (VQA)
- OCR and document parsing
- Chart and graph understanding
- Cross-modal reasoning (combining text and image context)

## Connections

- [[reasoning-models]] and multimodal capability are increasingly combined — e.g. Claude 4.7 and GPT-5.4
- [[google-deepmind]] Gemini 3.1 pairs multimodal with a 1M token context window
- [[meta-ai]] Llama 4 brings multimodal to open-weight models for the first time at flagship scale
