# LLM Landscape 2026

A snapshot of the structural dynamics shaping large language model development as of 2026.

## The Geographic Divide

Per [[sources/best-llms-2026-zapier]], the 2026 LLM landscape splits along geographic lines:

**Western companies → proprietary models**
- [[openai]] (US), [[anthropic]] (US), [[google-deepmind]] (US/UK), [[xai]] (US), [[cohere]] (Canada), Amazon (US), [[microsoft]] (US)
- Revenue models based on API access and enterprise contracts

**Chinese companies → open-weight models**
- [[deepseek]], [[alibaba-qwen]], Moonshot AI (Kimi), MiniMax, Xiaomi (MiMo), Z.ai (GLM)
- Open-weight releases as a competitive and political strategy

## Key Structural Shifts in 2026

1. **Open models match proprietary** — [[alibaba-qwen]] Qwen 3.5 Max and [[deepseek]] V3.2 benchmark at or above [[anthropic]] Claude 4.6 Opus and [[xai]] Grok 4
2. **Efficiency over raw compute** — [[deepseek]] demonstrated frontier results with less hardware and capital
3. **[[mixture-of-experts]] dominance** — virtually all large open models use MoE; parameter counts are no longer a useful comparison metric
4. **[[reasoning-models]] as standard** — extended inference is now expected in flagship models; not a differentiator
5. **Context window race** — [[xai]] Grok 4 (2M tokens), [[google-deepmind]] Gemini 3.1 and Amazon Nova 2 (1M tokens)
