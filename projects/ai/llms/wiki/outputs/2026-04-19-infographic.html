Open Models Now Match Proprietary

Qwen 3.5 Max "matches or exceeds models like DeepSeek V3.2, Grok 4, and Claude 4.6 Opus." The proprietary tier's moat is narrowing fast, driven entirely by Chinese open-weight labs releasing at frontier quality.

MoE is the Universal Architecture

8 flagship 2026 models use Mixture-of-Experts. Mistral 3 activates only 41B of 675B total parameters per token — about 6%. MoE makes direct parameter comparisons across models "impossible," per the wiki; active compute is the real metric.

Context Window Race at 2M Tokens

Grok 4 leads at 2 million tokens. Gemini 3.1 and Amazon Nova 2 both offer 1 million. The gap between the leader and second tier is already 2×. Whether extreme context windows deliver practical utility at scale is flagged as a knowledge gap in this wiki.

Reasoning is Now Table Stakes

Extended chain-of-thought inference ships in GPT-5.4, Claude 4.7, and Grok 4 on the proprietary side; and DeepSeek R1, Qwen 3.5 Max, GLM-5, Kimi K2.5, and MiniMax M2.5 on the open side. It is no longer a product differentiator — higher latency is the only cost.

The Geographic Split Is Strategic

Western labs monetise via API access and enterprise contracts. Chinese labs release open weights as "a competitive and political strategy" countering Western gatekeeping. Six Chinese labs — DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot, MiniMax, Xiaomi, Z.ai — now field frontier-class models independently.

DeepSeek Rewrote the Efficiency Story

DeepSeek "achieved state-of-the-art performance using more limited computer hardware and less financial investment than typical LLMs." This proved that frontier results no longer require frontier compute budgets — a finding that reframes the competitive dynamics of the entire landscape.
