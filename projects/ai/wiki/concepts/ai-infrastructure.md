# AI Infrastructure

The physical and computational foundation of AI: data centers, chips, energy, and water. As of 2026, the scale of AI infrastructure is raising serious environmental and geopolitical concerns.

## Scale (2026)
- **Global AI data center power draw**: 29.6 gigawatts — equivalent to New York City's peak demand
- **US data centers**: 5,427+ (leads globally by count)
- **Water**: GPT-4o annual water use may exceed the drinking water needs of 1.2 million people
- **China**: Leads in robotics deployments despite fewer data centers

## Geopolitical Dimension
- TSMC (Taiwan) and Samsung (South Korea) produce the most advanced AI chips
- Nvidia dominates AI accelerator market; US export controls limit Chinese access
- China accelerating domestic chip development (though still behind)
- Data center geography is national security geography

## Energy Implications
AI data centers are becoming a major driver of electricity demand:
- Hyperscale data centers (10 Breakthrough Technologies 2026 per MIT TR) require new grid infrastructure
- Renewable energy procurement race among major AI companies
- Grid stress in regions with high data center density
