# Agentic Commerce

The shift from AI as information retrieval (search) to AI as autonomous purchasing agent — AI systems that don't just help you find products but act on your behalf to research, compare, and complete transactions.

## Scale Projections
- **Salesforce (2026)**: AI drives $263B in online purchases in the 2026 holiday season — 21% of all orders
- **McKinsey (2030 projection)**: $3–5 trillion annually from agentic commerce

## Active Deployments (2026)
- **[[google-deepmind]]** Gemini Shopping Graph: can make calls to stores autonomously on user behalf
- **[[openai]]** ChatGPT Shopping: integrated with Walmart, Target, Etsy — users delegate purchasing decisions to the chatbot
- Traffic shift: web traffic from search engines and social media declining as AI chat captures consumer intent at the top of the funnel

## Why This Matters
Traditional e-commerce: user searches → browses → compares → decides → buys.
Agentic commerce: user states preference → agent searches, compares, recommends, and executes.

This collapses the funnel and shifts power from platforms (Google Search, Meta Ads) to AI assistants — whoever controls the agent controls the purchase decision.

## Risks
- Loss of consumer price comparison (agent chooses for you)
- Advertiser incentive to capture agent attention rather than human attention
- New liability questions: if agent makes a bad purchase, who is responsible?
