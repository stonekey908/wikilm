# Stanford HAI (Human-Centered AI Institute)

Stanford University's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence. Publishes the annual AI Index Report, the most comprehensive quantitative snapshot of AI's state across capability, adoption, economics, regulation, and societal impact.

## Key Facts
- Annual AI Index Report: tracks model performance, investment, regulation, employment, public opinion
- 2026 report co-authored by Yolanda Gil (USC computer scientist)
- Coverage includes US-China competition metrics, benchmark reliability, labor impacts, infrastructure footprint
