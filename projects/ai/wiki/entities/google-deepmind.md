# Google DeepMind

Google's consolidated AI research division (merger of Google Brain + DeepMind). Produces the Gemini model family and pioneering research in AI for scientific discovery.

## Key Facts
- Products: Gemini (including Gemini LLM used in AlphaEvolve)
- AlphaEvolve (2025): combined Gemini with evolutionary algorithms to discover new algorithms; applied to data center power optimization
- AlphaEvolve spawned open-source replications: OpenEvolve (Asankhaya Sharma), SinkaEvolve (Sakana AI), AlphaResearch
- Gemini Shopping Graph: can place store calls autonomously — major [[agentic-commerce]] play
- Ranked in top tier on Arena benchmark (behind Anthropic and xAI as of March 2026)
