# DeepSeek

Chinese AI lab that shocked the industry with its R1 model release (January 2025), demonstrating frontier-level performance achieved with significantly fewer resources than Western counterparts. The "DeepSeek moment" became an industry catchphrase for resource-efficient frontier AI.

## Key Facts
- R1 model (Jan 2025): matched Western frontier models at a fraction of the compute cost
- Open-weight release strategy made it immediately adoptable by US startups
- Part of the larger trend of Chinese labs closing the gap with Western AI
- Ranked just behind Anthropic, xAI, Google, OpenAI on Arena (per Stanford HAI 2026 index)

## Significance
- Forced a reassessment of Western assumptions about AI compute requirements
- Accelerated [[us-china-ai-competition]] narrative from "China is years behind" to "months behind, and closing"
- Open-weight release model pressured US closed-model companies
