# Alibaba (Qwen)

Chinese tech conglomerate whose AI research division produces the Qwen family of open-weight language models. Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct has 8.85 million downloads, making it one of the most widely adopted open-weight models globally as of early 2026.

## Key Facts
- Qwen model family: open-weight, highly customizable — preferred by US startups over closed US models for some use cases
- Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct: 8.85M downloads (most downloaded open-weight model at time of reporting)
- Part of the "Chinese open-weight" wave alongside [[deepseek]], Zhipu GLM, Moonshot Kimi

## Significance
- Demonstrates that open-weight Chinese models are becoming infrastructure for US products
- Challenges the assumption that the frontier = closed US models
