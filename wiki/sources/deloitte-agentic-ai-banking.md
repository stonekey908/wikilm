# Deloitte: Agentic AI in Banking

## Key Takeaways

**Definition of AI Agents**: Autonomous systems with "agency"—the ability to take initiative, reason independently, and execute actions toward defined outcomes. Built on LLMs and enhanced with [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] (RAG), they operate with minimal human supervision and can "make trade-offs, plan, act, and adapt in real time."

**Current State of Adoption**: While major tech platforms (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, Salesforce) are embedding agentic capabilities, actual banking deployments remain limited but emerging:
- **BNY Mellon**: Using agents for coding and payment validation
- **JPMorgan Chase**: Deployed LAW (Legal Agentic Workflows) achieving 92.9% accuracy on legal document queries
- **Payment firms** (Mastercard, PayPal): Experimenting with agentic commerce

**Three Implementation Pathways**:
1. **Smart Overlay**: Wrapping agents around existing processes without system overhauls (lowest risk, leverages current safeguards)
2. **Agentic by Design**: Building new autonomous applications from scratch with microservices (moderate risk/reward)
3. **Process Redesign**: Fundamentally restructuring workflows to maximize agent capabilities (highest risk, greatest potential)

## Critical Insights

**Transformation Required**: Moving from traditional RPA/automation requires "major overhauls" to banking processes, especially those with limited automation history. This is not a plug-and-play technology.

**Risk Management Non-Negotiable**: Key concerns include:
- Cybersecurity vulnerabilities
- Model reliability and [[autonomous-decision-making]] errors
- [[regulatory-compliance]] challenges
- Unintended agent behaviors ("rogue AI" scenarios causing financial system harm)
- Accountability gaps when agents operate autonomously

**Human Oversight Remains Central**: Despite automation potential, banks must "keep humans in key decision points to help ensure accountability, reduce risks, and bolster organizational resilience." The model is human-supervised agency, not full autonomy.

**Organizational Readiness**: Success depends on:
- Cultural shift from human-centric to human-guided operations
- [[governance-frameworks]] for agent behavior and oversight
- Risk expertise alongside technical capability
- Strategic selectivity—starting with "high-impact, lower-risk use cases"

**Third-Party Leverage**: Given constraints, vendor partnerships (Amazon Bedrock, Salesforce Agentforce, Google) offer practical near-term pathways.
