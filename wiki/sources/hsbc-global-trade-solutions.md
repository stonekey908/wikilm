# HSBC Global Trade Solutions Launch

## Overview

HSBC rebranded its Global Trade and Receivables Finance (GTRF) business as **Global Trade Solutions (GTS)** in June 2024. The rebranding reflects the bank's 155-year history in trade finance and its strategic positioning to support corporate treasurers navigating evolving global commerce.

## Three Strategic Pillars

1. **Global Connectors** — Help clients identify trade partners and markets, providing access to the broadest network of trading hubs
2. **Innovative Problem Solvers** — Deliver safer digital trade experiences with structured working capital solutions
3. **Strategic Partners** — Provide trusted guidance during growth, disruption, or uncertainty

## Scale & Market Position

- **$850 billion** in annual trade facilitated (2023 figure)
- Access to **90% of global trade flows**
- Network of **1.3 million businesses** across 50+ markets
- Positioned as **world's leading trade bank**

## Regional Growth Outlook

HSBC anticipates significant Asian market expansion. Key projection:
- Intra-Asian exports projected to rise from **$4.3 trillion (2023)** to **$7.1 trillion by 2030**
- Positioning GTS to capture this growth through expanded regional presence and connectivity

## Leadership Perspective

**Vivek Ramachandran**, Global Head of GTS, stated: "Through the power of our networks, HSBC's business customers can reliably access and connect to the biggest and fastest-growing trade hubs globally."

## Key Themes

- **Trade connectivity** as core competitive advantage in global commerce
- **Digital trade** as enabler for safer, more efficient transactions
- **Structured working capital solutions** as response to evolving corporate treasurer needs
- **Network scale** (1.3M businesses, 50+ markets) as differentiator in banking
- **Asian growth** as major strategic focus for GTS expansion
