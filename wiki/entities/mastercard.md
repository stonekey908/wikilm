# Mastercard

## Overview

Global payment technology company and network. Facilitates digital payments, card processing, and merchant services.

## Agentic AI Exploration ([[deloitte-agentic-ai-banking]])

Experimenting with **agentic commerce**—autonomous agents managing e-commerce transactions, customer interactions, and fraud detection.

## Significance

As a payments network, Mastercard's agentic commerce exploration suggests the technology is moving beyond individual bank operations into the infrastructure layer—affecting ecosystem-wide flows and interactions.
