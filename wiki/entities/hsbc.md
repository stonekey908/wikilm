# HSBC (HSBC Holdings plc)

## Overview

HSBC is a global banking and financial services organization with a 155-year history in trade finance. Positioned as the world's leading trade bank, HSBC facilitates approximately $850 billion in annual trade.

## Trade Finance Division

**Global Trade Solutions (GTS)** (rebranded June 2024):
- Formerly known as Global Trade and Receivables Finance (GTRF)
- Three strategic pillars: Global Connectors, Innovative Problem Solvers, Strategic Partners
- Network: 1.3 million businesses across 50+ markets
- Access to 90% of global trade flows

## Market Position

- **Scale**: $850B annual trade facilitation (2023)
- **Geographic reach**: 50+ markets globally
- **Strategic focus**: Asian trade growth (intra-Asian exports projected $4.3T → $7.1T by 2030)
- **Specialization**: Trade finance, working capital solutions, digital trade infrastructure

## Leadership

- **Vivek Ramachandran**: Global Head of Global Trade Solutions
