# HSBC (HSBC Holdings plc)

## Overview

HSBC is a global banking and financial services organization with a 155-year history in trade finance. Positioned as the world's leading trade bank, HSBC facilitates approximately $850 billion in annual trade.

## Trade Finance Division

**Global Trade Solutions (GTS)** (rebranded June 2024):
- Formerly known as Global Trade and Receivables Finance (GTRF)
- Three strategic pillars: Global Connectors, Innovative Problem Solvers, Strategic Partners
- Network: 1.3 million businesses across 50+ markets
- Access to 90% of global trade flows

## Market Position

- **Scale**: $850B annual trade facilitation (2023)
- **Geographic reach**: 50+ markets globally
- **Strategic focus**: Asian trade growth (intra-Asian exports projected $4.3T → $7.1T by 2030)
- **Specialization**: Trade finance, working capital solutions, digital trade infrastructure

## Leadership

- **Vivek Ramachandran**: Global Head of Global Trade Solutions (as of June 2024)
- **[[Tom Elliott]]**: Managing Director and COO of Global Trade Solutions (2025); leads digital transformation and modernization initiatives

## Operational Philosophy

[[HSBC]]'s trade finance transformation follows "power of big, culture of small" principle:
- **Power of Big:** Global resources, 22+ market presence, serving tens of thousands of clients
- **Culture of Small:** Local responsiveness, agility, innovation velocity enabled by strategic [[fintech-partnerships-in-banking]]

Rather than building all capabilities in-house, GTS partners with specialized fintech firms:
- [[Raft AI]] — process automation and workflow optimization
- [[Dowsure Technologies]] — embedded financing capabilities

## Digital Transformation (2025)

**Platform Replatforming:** Multi-year initiative migrating tens of thousands of clients across 22+ markets to modernized systems improving speed and accuracy.

**Flagship Product:** [[HSBC TradePay]]
- Launched late 2023
- Fully digital approval in under one minute
- $6bn in approved credit limits
- Client feedback: "fintech-like experience" — successful UX parity with modern digital-first competitors

**Five Strategic Focus Areas:**
1. Talent development
2. Commercializing digital investments
3. Continuous innovation in digital offerings
4. Supporting sustainable supply chain practices
5. Strengthening structured working capital solutions
