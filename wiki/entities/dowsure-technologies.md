# Dowsure Technologies

## Overview

Dowsure Technologies is a fintech company specializing in embedded financing capabilities. [[HSBC]] has invested in Dowsure Technologies as part of its strategic approach to [[Fintech Partnerships in Banking]], specifically to enhance embedded financing solutions within [[Global Trade Solutions]].

## Business Focus

- **Core Capability:** Embedded financing capabilities
- **Domain:** Trade finance and supply chain financing
- **Go-to-Market:** B2B partnerships enabling financial institutions to embed financing solutions

## HSBC Partnership

Rather than building embedded financing infrastructure entirely in-house, HSBC invested in Dowsure Technologies to accelerate capability deployment and market time. This reflects the broader [[fintech-partnerships-in-banking]] trend and HSBC's strategic philosophy of "power of big, culture of small."

**Use Case at HSBC:** Embedding financing options directly into customer workflows and platforms, enabling frictionless access to working capital without leaving the operational context.

## Strategic Position

Dowsure Technologies represents HSBC's commitment to "culture of small" innovation within "power of big" scale. By investing in specialized fintech firms, HSBC gains access to cutting-edge embedded finance technology without the overhead of internal R&D in a specialized domain.
