# Tom Elliott

## Overview

[[Tom Elliott]] is Managing Director and Chief Operating Officer (COO) of [[Global Trade Solutions]] at [[HSBC]], responsible for the strategic repositioning and modernization of the bank's trade finance division.

## Role and Responsibilities

- **Title:** Managing Director and COO of Global Trade Solutions
- **Organization:** [[HSBC]]
- **Focus:** Articulating and executing HSBC's trade transformation strategy, including the rebranding from GTRF to [[Global Trade Solutions]] and digital modernization initiatives

## Strategic Perspective

Elliott emphasizes HSBC's continued market dominance ("world's biggest trade bank") while acknowledging modern market pressures:
- [[Supply Chain Reconfigurations]] — geopolitical and logistical shifts requiring new solutions
- [[Geopolitical Shifts]] — trade dynamics influenced by political and regulatory changes
- [[Digital Acceleration]] — customer and competitive pressure for modern technology and UX

His articulation of the "power of big, but culture of small" operational philosophy reflects the challenge of balancing global scale with local market responsiveness.
