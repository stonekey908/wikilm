# Personal.ai

Personal.ai is a technology company focused on building AI digital twins for individual knowledge management.

## Focus

Personal.ai's core thesis: AI models can be trained to replicate and evolve with an individual's knowledge, creating a "digital twin" of a person's expertise, experience, and thought patterns.

## Technology Approach

- **Small Language Models (SLMs)** rather than general-purpose models
- **Personalization** — models trained on individual user data
- **Rapid retrainability** — ability to incorporate new information quickly
- **Privacy** — user ownership and control of personal data

## Publications & Research

- Published research on "AI Digital Twins: The Future of Personal Knowledge Management" (2026), discussing digital twins applied to personal knowledge preservation and cognitive enhancement

## Connection to Broader Themes

- Positioned as alternative to [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] for personal knowledge applications
- Related to [[second-brain]] and [[knowledge-management]] practices
- Extends the [[digital-twin]] concept from industrial/physical assets to individual knowledge
