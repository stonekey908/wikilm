# Raft AI

## Overview

Raft AI is a fintech company specializing in process automation and workflow optimization. [[HSBC]]'s [[Global Trade Solutions]] division has partnered with Raft AI as part of its strategic approach to [[Fintech Partnerships in Banking]].

## Business Focus

- **Core Capability:** Process automation and workflow optimization
- **Target Market:** Financial services and enterprise operations
- **Go-to-Market:** B2B partnerships with incumbent financial institutions

## HSBC Partnership

[[HSBC]] selected Raft AI as a strategic partner rather than building automation capabilities entirely in-house. This reflects the broader [[fintech-partnerships-in-banking]] trend where incumbents trade internal development friction for specialized fintech speed and domain expertise.

**Use Case at HSBC:** Trade finance process automation — likely applied to:
- Document processing and OCR
- Compliance and regulatory checking
- Deal workflow routing
- Approval automation

## Strategic Position

Raft AI exemplifies the "culture of small" part of HSBC's "power of big, culture of small" operational model: specialized, innovative partners handling specific technical domains while HSBC provides scale, distribution, and client relationships.
