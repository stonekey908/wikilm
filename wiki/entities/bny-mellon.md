# BNY Mellon

## Overview

Bank of New York Mellon. Global financial services firm specializing in asset management and payment systems.

## Agentic AI Deployment ([[deloitte-agentic-ai-banking]])

Using [[agentic-ai]] agents for:
- **Coding automation**: Autonomous code generation and validation
- **Payment validation**: Automated payment accuracy checking and fraud detection

## Significance

BNY Mellon represents practical adoption of agents in core banking operations (payments) and internal operations (coding). Demonstrates breadth of application—not just legal/document processing but operational and infrastructure domains.
