# Nielsen Norman Group

## Overview

Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) is a leading UX research and consulting firm founded by Jakob Nielsen and Don Norman, recognized for evidence-based design practices and human-centered research methodologies.

## Focus Areas

- **User Experience Research** — quantitative and qualitative studies, usability testing, accessibility
- **Human-Centered Design** — design principles grounded in psychological research and cognitive science
- **AI and Generative Applications** — emerging work on how AI and generative models can enhance or complement traditional UX research
