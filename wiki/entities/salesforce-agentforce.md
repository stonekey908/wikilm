# Salesforce Agentforce

## Overview

Salesforce enterprise software offering embedding [[agentic-ai]] into CRM and business workflows. Provides pre-built agents for customer service, sales, and operations automation.

## Role in Banking Adoption ([[deloitte-agentic-ai-banking]])

Identified as one of the practical vendor pathways for banks to adopt agentic capabilities, especially in customer-facing and operational workflows.

## Strategic Implication

Salesforce Agentforce and similar vendor solutions accelerate time-to-value for agentic automation but introduce vendor lock-in and third-party dependency risks that must be addressed in [[governance-frameworks]].

## Significance

Represents the broader trend of agentic AI moving from research/experimentation into productized, enterprise-ready offerings accessible to organizations without deep AI expertise.
