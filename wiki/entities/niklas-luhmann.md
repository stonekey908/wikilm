# Niklas Luhmann

German sociologist who maintained a famous system of handwritten note cards known as the [[zettelkasten]] (slip box).

## Relevance

Referenced in [[karpathy-second-brain-medium-article]] as a historical parallel to [[andrej-karpathy]]'s [[llm-wiki-pattern]]. Luhmann's key insight: the friction of writing something in your own words isn't wasted effort — it's the actual mechanism through which understanding happens. Reading someone else's summary is not the same as formulating the idea yourself.

This is cited as an important limitation of LLM-maintained knowledge bases: the AI handles the organizational drudgery, but the human still needs to do the thinking. The system compiles the territory; you still have to walk it.
