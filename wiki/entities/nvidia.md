# NVIDIA

## Overview

NVIDIA (NVIDIA Corporation) is a leading AI and computing company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Known for GPU (graphics processing unit) technology that became foundational to deep learning, NVIDIA has evolved from gaming graphics into a dominant AI infrastructure provider.

## Core Business

- **GPU Manufacturing**: CUDA-enabled processors for AI training and inference
- **AI Software Stack**: CUDA, cuDNN, TensorRT for optimized AI execution
- **Enterprise Platforms**: Accelerated cloud and data center infrastructure
- **AI Foundation Models**: Recent shift toward providing ready-to-deploy foundation models

## Key Products & Technologies

### GPU Hardware
- **H100, H200**: Latest data center GPUs for large-scale AI training
- **L40S, A100**: Specialized processors for inference and professional computing

### AI Platforms
- **NVIDIA Cosmos**: [[world-models]] and foundation models for [[physical-ai]] development
- **NVIDIA Omniverse**: Simulation and digital twin platform
- **NVIDIA RAPIDS**: GPU-accelerated data processing

### Software
- **CUDA**: Parallel computing platform enabling GPU acceleration
- **cuDNN**: Deep learning primitives library
- **TensorRT**: Inference optimization engine

## Role in [[Physical-AI]]

NVIDIA is a central player in [[physical-ai]] development:

1. **Hardware**: GPUs enabling training of massive world models
2. **Software**: CUDA ecosystem optimizing AI workloads
3. **Foundation Models**: [[nvidia-cosmos]] providing production-grade world models
4. **Simulation**: Omniverse platform for digital twin and robotics training

## [[World-Models]] Leadership

NVIDIA's [[nvidia-cosmos]] represents state-of-the-art world foundation models:
- **Tokenization**: Advanced visual tokenizers for efficient video/image representation
- **Model Types**: Prediction, style transfer, and reasoning models
- **Applications**: Autonomous vehicles, robotics, video analytics
- **Acceleration**: Purpose-built for GPU execution

## Market Position

- Dominant GPU supplier for AI infrastructure (est. 80%+ market share for training)
- Strategic partnerships with cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)
- Key supplier to autonomous vehicle and robotics companies
- Positioning as end-to-end [[physical-ai]] platform provider
