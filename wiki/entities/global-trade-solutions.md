# Global Trade Solutions (GTS)

## Overview

Global Trade Solutions (GTS) is the rebranded division of [[HSBC]] formerly known as Global Trade and Receivables Finance (GTRF). The rebranding signals a strategic shift from product-centric to solution-centric positioning, reflecting modern supply chain complexity, geopolitical volatility, and customer demand for digital-first experiences.

## Rebranding Context

**Old Name:** Global Trade and Receivables Finance (GTRF)
**New Name:** Global Trade Solutions (GTS)
**Timing:** 2025
**Rationale:** The shift from "receivables finance" (product) to "solutions" (outcome-focused) reflects customer needs evolving around:
- [[Supply Chain Reconfigurations]] — geopolitical and logistical reshuffling of global trade patterns
- [[Geopolitical Shifts]] — regulatory, political, and trade policy changes
- [[Digital Acceleration]] — customer expectations for modern, fast, user-friendly platforms

## Leadership

- [[Tom Elliott]] — Managing Director and COO

## Strategic Framework: "Power of Big, Culture of Small"

GTS operates on a hybrid model:
- **Power of Big:** Global resources, 22+ market presence, tens of thousands of clients
- **Culture of Small:** Local responsiveness, agility, and innovation velocity

Rather than building all solutions internally, GTS partners with fintech specialists:
- [[Raft AI]] — process automation and workflow optimization
- [[Dowsure Technologies]] — embedded financing capabilities

This approach reflects broader [[fintech-partnerships-in-banking]] trend where incumbent banks trade internal development friction for specialized fintech speed and innovation.

## Product Suite

### Documentary Credit
- Mitigates payment risks in cross-border transactions
- Improves cash flow management for trading parties
- Foundational traditional trade finance instrument

### TradePay
- GTS's flagship digital product, launched late 2023
- **"Faster, simpler digital trade finance solution"** enabling immediate supplier payments
- Fully digital approval in under one minute
- $6bn in approved credit limits
- Client feedback highlights "fintech-like experience" — success in UX parity with modern digital-first competitors

### Working Capital Optimization
- Faster liquidity access
- Capital efficiency unlocking for operations
- Supports growth and resilience

### International Trade Growth Solutions
- Supports market expansion initiatives
- Enables distribution network development

### Supply Chain Finance
- Strengthens supplier partnerships
- Builds liquidity across extended networks
- Integrated approach to multi-tier supplier financing

### Sustainable Trade Finance
- Helps businesses decarbonize supply chains
- Supports renewable energy initiatives
- ESG-aligned financing for climate goals

## Core Initiatives

### 1. Platform Replatforming
- Multi-year migration of tens of thousands of clients across 22+ markets
- Modernized backend systems improving processing speed and accuracy
- Result: Operational foundation for faster, more reliable service delivery

### 2. Strategic Focus Areas
1. **Talent Development** — Recruiting and retaining staff skilled in modern tech and business development

3. **Continuous Innovation** — Sustained product release velocity and feature parity
4. **Sustainable Supply Chain** — ESG-aligned financing solutions
5. **Structured Working Capital Solutions** — Integrated products for client retention

## Customer Challenges Addressed

GTS positions itself against six key enterprise pain points:

1. **Market Navigation** — Identifying and accessing optimal trading partners and hubs
2. **Supply Chain Resilience** — Building redundancy and flexibility into procurement
3. **Supplier Verification** — Risk assessment and relationship validation
4. **ESG Transitions** — Financing the shift to sustainable operations
5. **Working Capital Optimization** — Capital efficiency across extended supply chains
6. **Trade Documentation Burden** — Reducing friction and manual processes in cross-border transactions
