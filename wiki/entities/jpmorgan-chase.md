# JPMorgan Chase

## Overview

Largest bank in the United States by assets. Pioneer in financial services innovation and technology adoption.

## Agentic AI Deployment ([[deloitte-agentic-ai-banking]])

**LAW (Legal Agentic Workflow)**
- Autonomous agent system for legal document analysis and query answering
- Achieves **92.9% accuracy** across various legal queries
- Demonstrates practical success with [[agentic-ai]] in regulated banking domain
- Represents "delegated autonomy" model: agents decide with human oversight on exceptions

## Significance

JPMorgan Chase's LAW is cited as proof that [[agentic-ai]] is not theoretical for banking. Real deployments exist. However, 92.9% accuracy also illustrates the limitation: ~7% of decisions still require [[human-oversight]].

## Strategic Implication

JPMorgan is one of the "first movers" in agentic banking workflows, suggesting larger institutions with resources will lead adoption. Smaller banks may follow via [[governance-frameworks]] and best practices established by pioneers.
