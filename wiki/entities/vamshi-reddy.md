# Vamshi Reddy

Entrepreneur who responded to [[andrej-karpathy]]'s post about the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] with a notable observation about business applications.

## Notable Quote

*"Every business has a raw/ directory. Nobody's ever compiled it. That's the product."*

This captures the idea that every company has a mess of Slack threads, meeting transcripts, internal documents, product specs, and research that nobody can find or connect. The same wiki pattern works for all of it — competitive analysis, due diligence, trip planning, studying, and more.
