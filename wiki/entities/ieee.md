# IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)

## Who They Are

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for humanity. Founded in 1884, IEEE has over 400,000 members in 160+ countries. The organization:

- Sets technical standards for electrical, electronics, and computing technologies
- Publishes peer-reviewed research and technical journals
- Hosts conferences and professional development programs
- Provides resources for engineers, computer scientists, and technology professionals

## Publications & Influence

IEEE publishes:
- **IEEE Computer Magazine** — trends, research overviews, and industry analysis
- **IEEE Spectrum** — broader technology and society coverage
- Hundreds of specialized journals and conference proceedings
