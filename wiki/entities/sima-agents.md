# SIMA Agents

Scalable Instructable Multiworld Agents developed by [[google-deepmind]] for training [[agentic-ai]] systems. SIMA agents pursue complex, multi-step goals and have been tested in environments generated by [[genie-3]].

## Capabilities

- **Multi-Step Goal Pursuit**: Can decompose complex objectives into sequential actions
- **Multiworld Training**: Learn generalizable policies across diverse procedurally-generated environments
- **Instruction Following**: Respond to natural language task descriptions
- **Action Sequencing**: Extended action horizons for complex tasks

## Training Environment

- Tested and trained in [[genie-3]] environments
- Benefits from procedurally-generated world diversity
- Supports longer-horizon training scenarios

## Connection to [[Agentic-AI]]

SIMA agents represent practical implementation of [[agentic-ai]] principles:
- Autonomous reasoning about goals
- Multi-step planning and execution
- Adaptation to novel scenarios
- Scalability through diverse training environments
