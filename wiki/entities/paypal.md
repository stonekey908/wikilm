# PayPal

## Overview

Digital payments and fintech company. Facilitates money transfers, merchant payments, and e-commerce transactions globally.

## Agentic AI Exploration ([[deloitte-agentic-ai-banking]])

Experimenting with **agentic commerce**—similar to Mastercard's efforts, exploring autonomous agent management of transactions, fraud prevention, and customer service.

## Significance

Like [[mastercard]], PayPal's agentic commerce exploration reflects the trend toward [[agentic-ai]] systems at the payments layer, not just internal banking operations. Together, these two networks signal that agentic automation is entering the transaction infrastructure—not just enterprise back-office workflows.
