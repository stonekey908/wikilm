## Concepts
- [[llm-wiki-pattern]] — System where an LLM maintains a structured wiki from raw sources
- [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] — Chunk-and-search approach contrasted with the wiki pattern; core technique for agentic AI
- [[knowledge-compilation]] — Metaphor: raw research "compiled" into organized knowledge
- [[knowledge-management]] — Capturing, organizing, and applying knowledge; extends from Zettelkasten to modern wiki systems and AI models
- [[zettelkasten]] — Luhmann's handwritten note card system, historical parallel
- [[idea-files]] — Sharing specifications instead of code for LLM agents to build
- [[vibe-coding]] — AI-assisted programming style coined by Karpathy
- [[obsidian]] — Markdown note-taking app used as the wiki viewer
- [[digital-twin]] — Virtual replica of a physical asset synced to real-time data for simulation and optimization
- [[personal-language-models]] — Small, personalized language models trained on individual data; core technology in personal digital twins
- [[synthetic-user-modeling]] — AI models simulating how individuals or population segments respond to questions and design changes; encompasses UX research digital twins and broader user behavior prediction
- [[enterprise-metaverse]] — Interconnected digital twins forming an immersive enterprise environment
- [[predictive-maintenance]] — Data-driven maintenance strategy, top digital-twin use case
- [[agentic-ai]] — Autonomous AI systems with agency to reason, plan, execute, and adapt toward defined outcomes
- [[autonomous-decision-making]] — Systems making binding decisions without human pre-approval, spectrum from advisory to full autonomy
- [[governance-frameworks]] — Organizational structures defining what autonomous agents can do and how they're supervised
- [[human-oversight]] — Active human role monitoring, validating, and intervening in autonomous systems; essential control mechanism
- [[regulatory-compliance]] — Legal requirement that agent decisions and processes meet banking/sector regulations
