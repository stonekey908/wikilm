## Core Terms

- **[[llm-wiki-pattern]]** — System where an LLM ingests raw sources and maintains a structured, interlinked markdown wiki; value compounds with each ingest.
- **[[digital-twin]]** — Virtual replica of a physical asset synced to real-time sensor data; enables simulation, prediction, and optimization without touching the physical system.
- **[[agentic-ai]]** — AI systems that reason, plan, execute multi-step actions, and adapt autonomously toward defined outcomes — beyond simple Q&A or single-step automation.
- **[[world-models]]** — Neural networks that learn to simulate physical dynamics and predict consequences of actions; foundational layer for [[physical-ai]] and advanced [[digital-twin]] systems.
- **[[knowledge-compilation]]** — Metaphor for the LLM wiki ingest: raw research "compiled" into structured, interlinked knowledge — analogous to a compiler transforming source code.
- **[[retrieval-augmented-generation]]** — Chunk documents, embed them, retrieve similar chunks at query time; dominant industry pattern but produces no persistent structure or accumulated insight.
- **[[supply-chain-finance]]** — Liquidity optimization across multi-tier supplier *networks* (distinct from bilateral trade finance, which is buyer↔seller only).
- **[[governance-frameworks]]** — Organizational rules defining what autonomous agents may decide, escalate, or execute without human pre-approval; unsolved problem in both banking AI and agentic systems.

## What NOT to Confuse

1. **LLM wiki ≠ RAG.** RAG searches a document store at query time with no memory between runs. The wiki pattern *compiles* raw sources into persistent, structured, interlinked pages — it accumulates; RAG does not.

2. **Supply-chain finance ≠ trade finance.** [[trade-finance]] is bilateral (buyer-seller guarantees, letters of credit). [[supply-chain-finance]] optimizes liquidity across multi-tier supplier *networks* — a fundamentally different scope. HSBC GTS offers both but keeps them distinct.

3. **Digital twins ≠ dashboards / monitoring.** A digital twin models *behavior* — it simulates, predicts, and can run what-if scenarios. A monitoring dashboard reports current state. The [[ai-in-digital-twins-systematic-review]] exposes a persistent gap: many systems called "digital twins" lack true virtual-to-physical synchronization and are closer to dashboards.
