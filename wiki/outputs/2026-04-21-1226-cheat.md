## Core Terms

- **[[llm-wiki-pattern]]** — LLM-as-librarian: raw sources go in, structured interlinked markdown comes out. Three operations: ingest, query, lint. Pioneered by [[entities/andrej-karpathy]].
- **[[retrieval-augmented-generation]]** — Chunk → embed → search → answer at query time. Dominant industry approach; the wiki pattern argues it never accumulates.
- **[[digital-twin]]** — Live virtual replica of a physical asset synced to real-time (IoT) sensor data; includes behavioral insight and simulation layers, not just raw data.
- **[[agentic-ai]]** — Autonomous AI that reasons, plans, executes, and adapts toward defined outcomes with minimal human supervision. Distinct from RPA/traditional automation.
- **[[world-models]]** — Neural networks that learn physics and dynamics from video data to enable prediction and simulation of physical environments; foundation for [[physical-ai]].
- **[[supply-chain-finance]]** — Liquidity optimization across multi-tier supplier *networks* (vs. bilateral buyer-seller trade finance). Distinct product category in HSBC GTS stack.
- **[[zettelkasten]]** — [[entities/niklas-luhmann]]'s handwritten card index; historical parallel to the LLM wiki pattern. Key insight: friction of writing in your own words is the mechanism of understanding.
- **[[governance-frameworks]]** — Organizational structures defining what autonomous agents are permitted to do and how they are supervised; the unsolved problem in agentic banking deployment.

## What NOT to Confuse

1. **LLM wiki pattern ≠ RAG.** RAG retrieves chunks on demand and discards context; the wiki pattern compiles knowledge permanently into structured, interlinked pages that compound over time. Using both is possible — they solve different problems.

2. **Digital twin ≠ static 3D model or dashboard.** A true digital twin is *bidirectionally synced* to live sensor data and supports *simulation* ("what if" runs). A static model is a snapshot; a dashboard shows metrics. The [[sources/ai-in-digital-twins-systematic-review]] flags that most deployed "twins" still lack robust virtual-to-physical synchronization.

3. **Supply-chain finance ≠ trade finance.** Trade finance is bilateral (buyer-seller guarantees, letters of credit). Supply-chain finance optimizes liquidity *across entire supplier networks* — different product, different risk profile, different customer relationship.
