# Trade Finance

## Definition

Trade finance encompasses financial products and services that facilitate international and domestic trade transactions. Core functions include financing, payment guarantees, and risk management across global supply chains.

## Key Functions

1. **Financing** — Providing capital for trade transactions (working capital solutions)
2. **Payment guarantees** — Letters of credit, trade guarantees reducing counterparty risk
3. **Risk management** — Currency, credit, and operational risk mitigation
4. **Trade connectivity** — Network infrastructure enabling buyers and sellers to find and transact with counterparties

## Market Scale

According to [[hsbc]] (world's leading trade bank):
- **$850 billion** in annual global trade financed (2023)
- **90% of global trade flows** dependent on trade finance infrastructure
- **1.3 million businesses** actively participating in networked trade finance ecosystems

## Digital Trade

Emerging evolution toward **digital trade** — safer, more efficient trade transactions enabled by digitized documentation, payment, and verification processes. [[hsbc]]'s Global Trade Solutions emphasizes structured working capital solutions within this digital context.

## Regional Dynamics

Asian trade finance growth is particularly significant:
- **Intra-Asian exports** projected to grow from $4.3 trillion (2023) to $7.1 trillion (2030)
- Regional financial institutions positioning to capture this growth
