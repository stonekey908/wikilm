# Working Capital

## Definition

Working capital refers to the capital required to finance a business's day-to-day operations, including inventory, accounts receivable, and other short-term assets and liabilities. In trade finance context, working capital solutions provide financing to bridge payment timing gaps in supply chains.

## Role in Trade Finance

In global commerce, working capital management is critical:
- **Inventory financing** — Capital to acquire goods for resale or distribution
- **Receivables financing** — Advance payments against future customer payments
- **Trade credit** — Structured payment terms enabling suppliers and buyers to optimize cash timing
- **Structured solutions** — Tailored financing matching business cycle needs
