# Personal Language Models (PLMs)

Small, specialized language models trained on individual user data rather than general-purpose models. A core technology in [[personal-ai]]'s approach to [[digital-twin]] systems for personal knowledge management.

## Characteristics

1. **Personalized** — trained on an individual's unique data, knowledge, and thought patterns
2. **Small scale** — SLMs (Small Language Models) vs. LLMs (Large Language Models)
3. **Rapidly retrainable** — can quickly incorporate new information as users learn
4. **Controlled** — users retain full ownership and privacy over the model

## Advantages Over General-Purpose Models

| Aspect | General-Purpose LLM | Personal Language Model |
|--------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Accuracy | Broad but shallow | Deep and specific to user |
| Adaptation | Frozen after training | Continuously retrained |
| Privacy | Shared on company servers | User-controlled |
| Cost | Expensive API calls | Potential on-device training |

## Applications

- **Knowledge preservation** — models that grow with a person's expertise
- **Cognitive augmentation** — decision support informed by personal experience
- **Digital twins** — virtual representations of individual knowledge
- **Institutional memory** — applied at team/organization scale

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- **[[llm-wiki-pattern]]** — complementary; wikis provide structured data, PLMs provide continuous learning
