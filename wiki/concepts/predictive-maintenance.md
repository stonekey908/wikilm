# Predictive Maintenance

A maintenance strategy that uses real-time data and analytics to predict when equipment will fail, enabling intervention before breakdown occurs. One of the highest-value applications of [[digital-twin]] technology.

## How It Works with Digital Twins

A [[digital-twin]] continuously ingests sensor data from physical equipment. The analytics layer detects patterns and anomalies — such as vibration changes, temperature drift, or performance degradation — that signal impending failure. Maintenance is scheduled proactively rather than on fixed intervals (preventive) or after breakdown (reactive).

## Impact

Per [[mckinsey-what-is-digital-twin-technology]]:
- Up to **40% reduction in maintenance costs**
- Up to **10% increase in equipment uptime**
- Significant reduction in unplanned downtime and associated production losses

## Where It's Applied

Primarily in manufacturing, energy, aerospace, and any capital-intensive industry with expensive physical assets where downtime is costly.

## Relationship to Enterprise Scale

Predictive maintenance is the highest-ROI use case for individual [[digital-twin]] deployments. [[enterprise-metaverse]] extends this further — interconnecting twins across assets and organizations so failure prediction and maintenance schedules can be coordinated at network scale.
