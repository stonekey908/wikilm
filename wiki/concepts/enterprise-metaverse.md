# Enterprise Metaverse

A concept describing what emerges when an organization connects multiple [[digital-twin]]s across different business domains, functions, and operational ecosystems into a unified, immersive digital environment.

## What It Enables

- **Optimized decision making** from C-suite to front lines
- **Immersive experiences** for employees and customers via augmented and virtual reality
- **Autonomous AI use cases** not possible with isolated twins (e.g., self-healing equipment that proactively detects and resolves its own issues)
- **New product development** mechanisms and processes

## Distinction from Consumer Metaverse

The enterprise metaverse is focused on operational simulation and decision-making, not social interaction or gaming. It is grounded in real-time data from physical assets rather than purely virtual worlds.

## Current State

Per [[mckinsey-what-is-digital-twin-technology]], this remains a forward-looking concept — organizations are beginning to connect multiple twins, but full enterprise metaverse deployments are nascent. The foundation is individual digital twins proving value in their domains first.
