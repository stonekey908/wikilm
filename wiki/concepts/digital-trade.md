# Digital Trade

## Definition

Digital trade refers to the use of digital technologies, platforms, and infrastructure to facilitate, document, and execute international and domestic trade transactions. Encompasses digitized trade documentation, payment systems, verification processes, and connectivity networks.

## Strategic Importance

**Safer transactions**: Digital infrastructure reduces fraud risk through verified, transparent documentation and payment flows.

**Efficiency**: Automation of trade documentation, customs clearance, and payment reduces transaction time and operational costs.

**Connectivity**: Digital platforms enable businesses to discover trade partners and market opportunities at scale.
